Rustmix-Wave native Dice sample: shake the device to roll.

One to three six-sided dice sit on the table. SELECT rolls them, or
shake the device when Shake to roll is on. BOOT opens Options (dice
count, sound, shake and shake strength); hold BOOT leaves.

  SELECT roll
  UP/DOWN change the number of dice (from Ready)
  BOOT (short) Options
  BOOT (hold) back to Games

A roll is three quick frames with the sound of real dice and a knock
where they land. A full refresh every 10 rolls clears ghosting. The
last faces are saved to RECORDS.TXT as dice_last when you leave.

NOTE: the firmware that wires Dice is not released yet. Until then this
app is listed in the Games hub but cannot be played.
